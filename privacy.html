Privacy policy

Hermes (personal) is a private assistant with one user: its owner. This policy covers the Google data it accesses.

What it accesses

Read-only access to the owner's Gmail (gmail.readonly) and Google Calendar, including calendars shared with the owner (calendar.readonly). It cannot send, modify, delete or create email or calendar events.

How the data is used

The data is read on the owner's own server and processed through the Anthropic API (Claude) to produce summaries, such as a daily briefing, for the owner only.

Sharing

The data is not sold, and it is not shared with anyone else. Apart from Anthropic, which processes it to produce the summaries, it isn't disclosed to any third party, and it isn't used for advertising.

Storage and removal

Access tokens are stored on the owner's server with owner-only permissions. Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account's security settings (Third-party apps & services).
